# Code of Conduct

QuietDo welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Project spaces should remain constructive, respectful, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical decisions with evidence.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's task data or credentials.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/weieast1314/quietdo/security/advisories/new). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
